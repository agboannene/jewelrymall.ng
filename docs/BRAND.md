## 1. Brand Foundation

### 1.1 Brand Essence
**Affordable luxury that travels from DM to doorstep — trusted, radiant, and unmistakably Nigerian.**

### 1.2 Mission
To make beautiful jewelry easy to find, fairly priced by quantity, and confidently bought — whether you need one pair for yourself or one hundred for your customers.

### 1.3 Vision
Nigeria's most trusted jewelry mall — online first, WhatsApp close, quality consistent.

### 1.4 Values
| Value | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| **Radiance** | Every touchpoint should feel polished, bright, and worth sharing. |
| **Clarity** | Prices, packs, and policies are explicit. No fine-print anxiety. |
| **Trust** | Verified payments, honest stock, no fake discounts. |
| **Warmth** | Conversations sound like a helpful friend who knows jewelry. |
| **Access** | Wholesale is not exclusive — quantity simply unlocks better pricing. |

### 1.5 Brand Promise
> *What you see is what you get — correct price, real stock, kind help, delivered as promised.*

## 2. Positioning & Audience

### 2.1 Positioning Statement
For **young women and resellers in Nigeria** who love expressive jewelry but are tired of price confusion and DM stress, **JewelryMallNG is the online mall** that shows every colour, pack, and quantity price clearly, lets you verify and pay safely, and answers instantly on WhatsApp — because our website, dashboard, and assistant share the same truth.

### 2.2 Audience Map

| Segment | Who | What They Value | Brand Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Retail Muse** (60% revenue) | 18-34, students / young professionals, IG/TikTok native | Aesthetic, affordability, fast answers, proof of quality | Feel beautiful, confident, and seen |
| **Wholesale Hustler** (35% revenue) | 25-45, market traders, IG vendors, bulk buyers | MOQ clarity, tier savings, mixed packs, reliable restock | Feel profitable and supported |
| **Gift Giver** (5%) | Partners, friends, families | Curated sets, gift packaging, delivery certainty | Feel thoughtful without effort |

### 2.3 Competitive Frame
- **Not:** A single-designer boutique (too narrow), a faceless import catalogue (no trust), or a chaotic DM store (no system).
- **Is:** A **mall** — curated variety, clear stalls, central checkout.

### 2.4 Cultural Context (Nigeria, 2026)
Mobile-first, data-conscious, WhatsApp as operating system, video-driven discovery, price sensitivity + P2P trust economy. Brand must feel local premium — not imported, not informal.

## 3. Brand Personality & Archetype

**Primary Archetype: The Lover + The Magician (refined)**
- Lover: beauty, intimacy, adornment, self-expression.
- Magician: transformation — how the right earring changes an outfit, a pack changes a business.

**Personality Sliders:**
```
Playful ●──────────○ Dignified  → Leans dignified (65%)
Casual  ●────────○ Formal     → Warm-formal (polite, not stiff)
Trendy  ●──────○ Timeless   → Timeless with trendy highlights (70/30)
Accessible ●────○ Exclusive → Accessible-luxe (price ladder, look premium)
Soft ●────────○ Bold       → Soft-bold (approachable but confident)
```

**Character in one line:** *Your stylish big sister who sells jewelry and always knows what's in stock.*

## 4. Voice & Messaging

### 4.1 Voice Principles

| Principle | Sounds Like | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| **Warm & Helpful** | "Let's find your perfect match — gold or silver?" | Cold, scripted |
| **Clear & Confident** | "12 pcs mixed colours — ₦1,200 each. MOQ 12." | Vague, "DM for price" |
| **Radiant, not Loud** | Light sparkle, not shouting CAPS or emojis flood | Hype, slang overload |
| **Honest** | "That variant is low — only 3 left." | Fake urgency |

### 4.2 Tone Ladder

| Context | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| PDP / Price Table | Crisp & factual | "Wholesale: ₦1,200 each (12-29 pcs). Retail: ₦1,800 (1-11 pcs)." |
| WhatsApp AI | Warm, concise, helpful | "Hi Ada! I found 3 pearl studs close to your photo. Want gold or mixed?" |
| Error / OOS | Empathetic, solution-oriented | "This colour is out, but the rose-gold is identical — or I can alert you when it's back?" |
| Policy | Reassuring | "Pay securely via Paystack. We only mark paid after verification." |

### 4.3 Messaging Pillars
1.  **Clarity is Luxury** — Every price and pack explained upfront.
2.  **One Mall, Every Style** — From studs to sets, retail to wholesale.
3.  **WhatsApp Close** — Real answers, real links, human when needed.
4.  **Trust You Can Verify** — Stock, payment, order status — all proven.

### 4.4 Taglines
- **Master:** *Shine Clearly.*
- **Alternatives (contextual):** *Your Mall for Every Sparkle.* / *From One to One Hundred.* / *Beautiful, Priced Right.*

### 4.5 Hashtag & CTA Lexicon
- Hashtags: #ShineClearly #JewelryMallNG #FromOneToOneHundred
- CTAs: `Shop the Look`, `Check Wholesale Price`, `Chat on WhatsApp`, `See It On`, `Add to Pack`

## 6. Visual Identity — Logo System

### 6.1 Concept (Recommendation: "Radiant Mall Mark")
A luxury-meets-mall mark that works at 24px favicon and on a billboard.

- **Wordmark:** Modern serif for "JEWELRYMALL" (elegance) + geometric sans for "NG" badge (structure/mall).
- **Icon / Monogram:** Interlocked **J + M** forming a diamond/lozenge with a subtle sparkle star cutout. Negative space suggests a storefront arch/mall entrance.
- **Why it wins:** Reads as jewelry (diamond), mall (arch), and sparkle (star) in one.

### 6.2 Logo Suite (Deliverables)

| Lockup | Use | Min Size |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary:** Icon above "JEWELRYMALL / NG" centered | Website header (desktop), packaging, social profile | 32mm print / 120px digital |
| **Secondary:** Icon left + wordmark horizontal | Website header (mobile sticky), invoices, packing list | 28mm / 100px |
| **Icon alone:** Lozenge J+M | Favicon, app icon, highlight covers, watermarks | 16px |
| **Wordmark alone:** Text only | Watermark contexts | — |

Each with **Light** (on white/cream) and **Dark** (on deep plum/charcoal) versions + single-colour.

### 6.3 Clear Space & Sizing
- Clear space = `x` where `x` = height of the "J" in the monogram. No element intrudes.
- Minimum sizes enforced; below, use icon alone.

### 6.4 Logo Misuse
Do not rotate, stretch, add drop shadow, recolour arbitrarily, place on busy photo without contrast card, or pair with competing fonts.

## 7. Colour Palette

### 7.1 Strategic Palette — "Midnight Sparkle"
Luxury without alienating. Deep plum grounds the gold; blush softens; cream keeps it airy on mobile.

| Role | Name | Hex | Usage | WCAG |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary** | Midnight Plum | `#2B0F2B` | Headers, nav, primary buttons, footer | Text on Cream 15.2:1 ✓ |
| **Accent** | Radiant Gold | `#C9A86A` | CTAs (gradient), highlights, sparkle star, price tier badge | Not for body text alone; pair with Plum |
| **Accent 2** | Blush Rose | `#E8AFAF` / `#D99A9A` | Hover states, wholesale nudge, secondary badges | — |
| **Neutral Light** | Pearl Cream | `#FFF7F0` / `#FDF6EE` | Page background, cards | — |
| **Neutral Dark** | Charcoal Ink | `#1A1A1E` | Body text | On Cream 16:1 ✓ |
| **Success** | Emerald Verify | `#1B7A5A` | "Paid • Verified", in-stock | — |
| **Warning** | Amber Low | `#C98A1A` | Low-stock, MOQ nudge | — |
| **Danger** | Garnet Alert | `#9B2C2C` | OOS, error | — |

### 7.2 Colour Proportions (60-30-10)
- 60% Pearl Cream / White
- 30% Midnight Plum
- 10% Radiant Gold + Blush (accents)

### 7.3 Gradients (Use Sparingly)
- Gold foil: `linear(135deg, #C9A86A 0%, #E8C99A 50%, #C9A86A 100%)` — only for primary CTA shimmer on hover and hero accents.
- Plum dusk: `linear(180deg, #2B0F2B 0%, #1A0A1A 100%)` — hero/footer.

### 7.4 Dark Mode (Future)
Invert: Plum background, Cream text, Gold remains accent. WCAG AA maintained.

## 8. Typography

### 8.1 Type System — Editorial Luxury × Functional

| Role | Typeface (Recommended) | Rationale | Fallback |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Display / Logo** | **Canela** or **Cormorant Garamond** (serif) | High contrast, jewelry elegance, editorial luxury | Georgia |
| **Body / UI** | **General Sans** or **Inter** (geometric/humanist sans) | Legible on small screens, tabular numbers for prices | System Sans |
| **Accent / Badge** | **Space Grotesk** (optional) | Wholesale/mall structure feel | Inter |

*Alternatives if licensing constrained: Playfair Display (display) + DM Sans (body) — both Google Fonts, zero cost.*

### 8.2 Type Scale (Major Third, 1.250)

| Style | Size (Desktop) | Size (Mobile) | Weight | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Display | 48/48 | 32/32 | 500 | Hero headlines |
| H1 | 36/40 | 28/32 | 600 | Page titles |
| H2 | 28/32 | 22/26 | 600 | Section heads |
| H3 | 20/28 | 18/24 | 600 | Card titles |
| Body | 16/26 | 15/24 | 400 | Descriptions |
| Small | 14/20 | 13/18 | 400 | Captions, tier tables |
| Label | 12/16 | 11/14 | 600 uppercase 0.08em | Badges, "WHOLESALE" |
| Price | 18/24 | 16/20 | 700 | Tabular numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) |

### 8.3 Typesetting Rules
- Line length 45-75 chars; paragraph spacing 0.75em.
- Prices always tabular, with `₦` preceded by narrow space (`₦ 1,200`).
- Never set body in gold; always Plum/Charcoal on Cream.

## 9. Imagery & Art Direction

### 9.1 Photography Style — "Soft Luxury, Real Skin"
- **Lighting:** Soft, diffused daylight + subtle gold reflector; no harsh flash.
- **Skin:** Inclusive, Nigerian skin tones prioritised; texture retained (no plastic retouch).
- **Context:** 70% on model (ear/neck close-ups, lifestyle), 30% flat-lay on Pearl Cream or soft linen.
- **Props:** Minimal — pearl, linen, brushed gold tray. Avoid clutter.
- **Crop:** Square 1:1 for catalog (consistency), 4:5 for IG, 16:9 for hero.
- **Post:** Warm white balance (+3), lifted shadows, gold stays true (`#C9A86A` reference).

### 9.2 Video / Reels Direction
- 5-12s loops, macro sparkle, hand movements, pack unboxing. Add subtle shimmer overlay (brand pattern) at 8% opacity.

### 9.3 Image Do / Do Not
- **Do:** Consistent background, sharp focus on product, scale reference (on ear/hand).
- **Do Not:** Neon filters, heavy vignette, watermark over product, inconsistent shadows.

## 10. Iconography & Patterns

### 10.1 Icon Style
- Outline, 1.5px stroke, rounded caps, Plum primary, Gold for active. 20/24px grid. Library: Lucide or custom.
- Key icons: diamond, pack/box, price tag, WhatsApp bubble, truck, shield-verify.

### 10.2 Pattern — "Lozenge Grid"
- Repeat of the J+M lozenge at 5% opacity on Pearl Cream, used for packing list header, gift card background, loading shimmer.
- Sparkle star (4-point) as bullet and list marker.

## 11. Motion & Sound Cues

### 11.1 Motion (150-250ms, ease-out)
- **Micro:** Button hover — gold shimmer sweep; card hover — 4px lift + soft shadow.
- **Page:** Staggered fade-in for product grids (`24ms` stagger).
- **Price change:** Gentle count-up (300ms) when tier flips to reinforce "savings unlocked".
- **Never:** Bouncy or slow (feels cheap).

### 11.2 Sound (Optional, for WhatsApp alerts)
- Staff notification: soft chime (G major triad, 0.4s) — not intrusive.

## 12. Application System

### 12.1 Website (Primary Touchpoint)
- **Header:** Plum bar, Cream wordmark, Gold cart badge; sticky on scroll with reduced height.
- **Hero:** Cream background, serif headline "Shine Clearly", Gold CTA `Shop Now →`, lozenge pattern ghost.
- **Product Card:** White card, 12px radius, image 1:1, Blush "WHOLESALE SAVE" badge when qty tier met, price tabular.
- **Tier Table:** Pill tabs for qty ranges; active pill = Plum bg, Cream text.
- **Cart Drawer:** Slides from right, Plum header, packing list CTA.
- **Footer:** Plum dusk gradient, Gold dividers, newsletter (WhatsApp opt-in instead of email spam).

### 12.2 Social (IG/TikTok)
- **Profile image:** Plum circle, Gold monogram centered, Cream outer ring.
- **Highlight covers:** 6 templates (New In, Packs, Wholesale, Reviews, FAQs, Contact) — Plum bg, Gold icon, Cream label.
- **Story templates:** 1080×1920, top/bottom safe zones, price sticker style: `₦ 1,200 EACH (12+)` in Gold pill.
- **Post watermark:** Small icon at bottom-right, 12% opacity.

### 12.3 WhatsApp
- **Display name:** JewelryMallNG
- **About line:** "Shine Clearly ✨ | Retail & Wholesale | Tap to shop: jewelrymallng.com"
- **Greeting template:** "Hi {{1}}! Looking for studs, sets, or wholesale packs? Send a photo or tell me colour & qty — I'll share links with correct prices."
- **Quick replies:** Buttons: `View Catalogue`, `Check Order`, `Talk to Person`.

### 12.4 Print & Operational (Minimal)
- **Packing list:** Plum header with monogram, Gold rule, tabular items.
- **Invoice:** Same + verified stamp (Emerald).
- **Business card (future):** Plum front (Gold foil logo), Cream back.

## 14. Brand in Product & Digital UX

### 14.1 UX Writing (Brand Meets Function)
- Price display pattern: `₦ 1,200 /pc (12+)` + strikethrough retail when wholesale unlocked.
- Stock copy: `In stock • 42 left` (Emerald dot), `Low • Only 3 left` (Amber), `Out — Notify me` (Garnet).
- Empty states: Friendly + action: "Your pack is empty. Add 6 beauties to unlock wholesale!"

### 14.2 Trust Signals (Jewelry-Specific)
- "Verified Paystack" + "7-day policy" chips near Add to Cart.
- Material disclosure: "Gold-plated • Hypoallergenic • Tarnish-resistant" where applicable.
- Real reviews with photos prioritised.

## 15. Brand Guardrails — Do / Do Not

| Do | Do Not |
|---|---|
| Use Pearl Cream as base; let Plum anchor | Use pure black or neon backgrounds |
| Keep gold as 10% accent — precious | Flood page with gold (cheap) |
| Photography with true skin tones | Over-brighten or distort colours |
| Uppercase labels only for badges (12px) | Uppercase whole paragraphs |
| One sparkle star per section max | Pattern everywhere |
| Rounded 12-16px corners, soft shadows | Sharp corners + heavy drop shadows |

## 16. Discovery Process & Identity Milestones

Per Build Plan §5, follow this:

| Step | Activity | Deliverable | Approval |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Brand Brief (Day 1-2) | Audience, personality, competitors, visual goals workshop (60 min) | Brief doc + moodboard (2 directions) | Owner sign-off |
| 2. Directions (Day 3-4) | Present 2 distinct directions (Mood A: Midnight Luxe vs Mood B: Soft Mall) | Figma boards | Choose 1 direction |
| 3. Refinement (Day 5-7) | Logo suite, colours, type, pattern, social kit | Logo pack + mini guide (8 pages) | Final approval |
| 4. Application (Day 8+) | Apply to website, favicon, packing list, WhatsApp profile | Live site + assets zip | QA check |

**Decision:** Recommend **Direction A: Midnight Sparkle** (described above) as starting point — balances luxury + mall accessibility better than pastel alternative for Nigerian market where trust = premium.
